## Course description

The course introduces financial statements and the financial-reporting process from a user’s perspective. It focuses on the principles and rules governing communication between firms and their stakeholders, with the goal of developing the ability to read, interpret, and use corporate financial information.

## Materials

- Readings, lecture handouts, prerecorded materials, practice exercises, case studies, slides, and articles are distributed through Canvas.
- Textbook: *Financial Accounting*, Libby, Libby, and Hodge, 11th edition.

## Assessments

### Final exam

- Three hours, in class during the normal meeting time in final-exam week.
- Closed book and closed notes; a calculator is allowed and recommended.
- The exam schedule will not change and make-up exams are not available.
- Section 06 exam: Friday, December 11, during the regular 1:30–4:30 p.m. class time.

### In-class mini exercises

- Five closed-book, closed-note exercises in Weeks 3, 5, 6, 8, and 9.
- The final mini-exercise grade uses the best three of five scores.
- No make-up mini exercises are offered.

### Cases

- The course covers nine cases. Every student should prepare every case and may be called on.
- Students work in teams and each group presents one assigned case.
- Only the presenting group submits slides and a written solution, both due before the relevant class begins.
- The grade includes a common group score and an individual score based on each student’s contribution and responses to questions.
