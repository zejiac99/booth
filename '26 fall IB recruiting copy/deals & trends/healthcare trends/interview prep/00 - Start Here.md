## How to use the packets in a conversation

For your chosen personal trend story, use Packet 1's 90–120-second answer when the conversation allows that context. Lead with its thesis so an interruption still leaves the main point clear. The shorter openers remain available; pause after a standalone deal pitch and let the banker choose the follow-up.

A 90-second or two-minute conversation can cover the mechanism, a transaction, and your opinion. For the preferred payer story, the order is: strategic logic of vertical integration → earlier platform acquisitions → operating expectations reset → recent acquisitions and alternative ownership structures → your view and Humana connection.

Rehearse the pitches aloud. The timings are estimates, not recording measurements; individual openers are generally around 65–80 words. Keep the personal sentences accurate to your actual role.
